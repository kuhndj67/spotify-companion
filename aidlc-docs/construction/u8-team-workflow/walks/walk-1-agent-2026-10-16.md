**Gap found in S8's wording**: the step says to note the time and *then* let a track finish. If a track is already
playing when the time is noted, which is the natural case because the person has just started music for
`/api/now-playing`, that track's `startedAt` comes **before** the noted time. Here *Masterpiece* started at 20:31:58,
seven seconds before the noted 20:32:05. Letting it finish, which is what a literal reader would do, would fail
*Expected* even though the app works. The agent told the person to skip to the next track and let *that* one finish.
In the end, *Masterpiece* played out and the following track also finished before the check. The document should say
to note the time **before** the track you will let finish **starts**, or to use the next track.

**Matched Expected?** **No**, as written. The results match, but getting a valid result needed the reasoning above,
which the step does not give. The page details are also only covered by the person's general report.
